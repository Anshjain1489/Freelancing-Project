# Client Production Demonstration & Sales Script 🎙️🏪

## Demo Overview
This script is designed for presenting the Chaudhary Kirana Store platform to potential store owner clients or buyers.

## Demonstration Flow (15 Minutes)

### 1. Customer Online Shopping & PWA Experience (3 mins)
- Open Customer Storefront homepage. Point out white-label branding, logo, search bar, category navigation, and responsive design.
- Demonstrate adding items to cart, viewing cart subtotal, and proceeding through guest or authenticated checkout.
- Highlight PWA offline fallback behavior.

### 2. POS Counter Billing & Fast Cashier Operations (3 mins)
- Navigate to `/admin/pos`. Demonstrate rapid item barcode/search selection, GST breakdown calculation, discount application, cash/online payment collection, and instant thermal receipt printing.

### 3. Inventory Management, WAC Costing & Procurement (3 mins)
- Navigate to `/admin/procurement`. Show purchase order generation, supplier management, weighted average cost (WAC) tracking, stock intake, and stock adjustment logs.

### 4. Financial Accounting, Expenses & Profit & Loss (3 mins)
- Navigate to `/admin/finance`. Display Net Sales, COGS, Operating Expenses, Net Profit, Cash Session Reconciliation, and Supplier Payables.

### 5. White-Labeling & System Health Console (3 mins)
- Navigate to `/admin/store-configuration`. Show instant primary color modification, store name change, and module feature flag toggling (`ENABLE_POS`, `ENABLE_DELIVERY`).
- Show `/admin/system-health` and operational readiness probes.
